#This folder contains:
(1) Survey_Questionnaire.pdf: Survey questionnaire used for the nationwide expert opinion survey on component restoration models of highway bridges in China. 
(2) Information_GM.pdf: Information of the adopted ground motions in finite element analyses of the case study for resilience assessment of a typical highway bridge against Design Basis Earthquakes characterized by peak ground accelerations of 0.4g. The acceleration time history data of the ground motions can be downloaded from the PEER Strong Motion Database (ngawest2.berkeley.edu) according to the provided information (e.g., record sequence numbers).

#The related paper submitted to Earthquake Spectra is as below:
Wang X., Ye A., Yang D., Wu X., Zhou L., Song K., Li J., Peng J., Lou L., Wei X. (202X) “Component restoration models of highway bridges for resilience assessment: A nationwide expert-opinion survey study and application.” Earthquake Spectra. Submission ID: EQS-24-0027R1.
