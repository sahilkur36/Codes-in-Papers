This folder contains:

(1) **Survey_Questionnaire.pdf**: Survey questionnaire used for the nationwide expert opinion survey on _component restoration models_ of highway bridges in China. 

(2) **Information_GM.pdf**: Information of the adopted ground motions in finite element analyses of the case study for resilience assessment of a typical highway bridge against Design Basis Earthquakes characterized by peak ground accelerations of 0.4g. The acceleration time history data of the ground motions can be downloaded from the [PEER Strong Motion Database](https://ngawest2.berkeley.edu) according to the provided information (e.g., record sequence numbers).

The related paper submitted to Earthquake Spectra:

Wang X., Ye A., Yang D., Wu X., Zhou L., Song K., Li J., Peng J., Lou L., Wei X. (2024) “Component restoration models of highway bridges for resilience assessment: A nationwide expert-opinion survey study and application.” Earthquake Spectra (Online First). https://doi.org/10.1177/87552930241290487
